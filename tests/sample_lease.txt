RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement is entered into as of January 1, 2026, between
Sunrise Property Group ("Landlord") and Jordan Lee ("Tenant") for the premises
located at 412 Maple Avenue, Apartment 3B.

1. TERM. The lease begins on January 1, 2026 and ends on December 31, 2026.
Unless Tenant provides written notice at least 60 days before the end of the term,
this lease automatically renews for successive one-year periods.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay rent of $1,850 per month, due on the first calendar day
of each month. A late fee of $75 plus interest of 5% per month shall apply to any
payment received after the fifth day of the month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $2,400. Landlord may
apply the deposit to unpaid rent, cleaning, or any claimed damages without prior
notice.

4. UTILITIES. Tenant is responsible for electricity, internet, and a $45 monthly
"administrative services" fee, which is non-refundable.

5. EARLY TERMINATION. If Tenant vacates before the end of the term, Tenant remains
liable for all remaining rent through the original end date, plus a $1,000 early
termination fee. Landlord may re-rent the unit without reducing Tenant's remaining
balance.

6. LIABILITY. Tenant agrees to indemnify and hold Landlord harmless from any
claims arising from use of the premises, including claims caused in whole or in
part by Landlord's negligence, except where prohibited by law.

7. DISPUTE RESOLUTION. Any dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration in
the county selected by Landlord. Tenant waives the right to a jury trial and to
participate in a class action.

8. ACCESS. Landlord may enter the premises at any time without notice for any
reason Landlord deems appropriate.

9. GOVERNING LAW. This agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which
the premises are located.

10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This document is the entire agreement. Verbal promises
are not enforceable.
